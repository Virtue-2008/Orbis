Technical Specification: ORBIS v12.0 Synthetic Physics Holdout Evaluation & Methodology

Document Status: FROZEN (v12.0.0-GOLDEN)

Target Audience: Institutional Reviewers, Risk Engineers, Scientific Advisors

Core Objective: Establish baseline performance under controlled synthetic conditions and document freeze policy prior to empirical validation.

1. Executive Summary & Freeze Policy

The ORBIS Wildfire Ignition Risk Engine (v12.0) core model architecture and weight matrices are officially frozen. Model tuning based on synthetic holdouts is concluded to prevent overfitting and score-chasing.

The evaluation lifecycle follows a strict sequence:

$$\text{Model Development} \longrightarrow \text{Frozen Baseline} \longrightarrow \text{Synthetic Evaluation} \longrightarrow \text{Empirical Field Validation}$$

2. Synthetic Holdout Dataset Generation & Modeling Assumptions

To evaluate model behavioral stability without data leakage, the pipeline generates an independent synthetic dataset ($N = 2,500$) grounded in micro-climate physics and terrain dynamics.

Feature Derivation

Input variables (slope, aspect, temperature, relative humidity, wind speed) are transformed into physics-based features:

Equilibrium Moisture Content (EMC): $EMC = 21.06 - 0.48(RH) - 0.00035(RH \cdot T)$
Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD): Derived via saturated vapor pressure curves.
Wind Channeling ($w_{\text{channeled}}$): Slope-adjusted aspect vectoring.
Hot-Dry-Wind Index (HDW): Interactive product of VPD and channeled wind.
Ground-Truth Probability Generation
Raw Latent Hazard: Calculated via weighted feature contrast vectors.
Z-Score Standardization: Standardized to zero mean and unit variance ($\mu = 0, \sigma = 1$) to stabilize variance across climate profiles.

Prevalence Parameterization: The synthetic holdout was parameterized with an approximately 15% positive-event prevalence to represent a low-frequency, high-consequence classification setting:

$$p_{\text{true}} = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-(3.2 \cdot Z - 1.95)}}$$

3. Calibrated Performance Metrics & Calibration Method

Raw model probabilities are calibrated using Maximum Likelihood Platt Scaling against the holdout outcomes.

4. Scientific Claims & Public Disclaimer Guidelines

To maintain scientific integrity in external documentation and institutional presentations:

Approved Public Statement: "In an independently generated synthetic physics holdout ($N=2,500$), ORBIS v12.0 achieved an ROC-AUC of 0.8048, Brier score of 0.1352, and log-loss of 0.4230 under a 15% positive-event prevalence constraint."
Required Mandatory Disclaimer: "These results assess the model's behavior under controlled synthetic conditions and should not be interpreted as empirical wildfire prediction performance."
Prohibited Phrasing: Never claim ORBIS is "scientifically proven accurate," "100% predictive," or "validated against real-world ignitions." Internal terms like "Golden Band Reached" refer exclusively to target optimization parameters within synthetic benchmarks.

5. Post-Freeze Roadmap: Transition to Empirical Validation

Version Lock: Commit engine binaries under Git tag v12.0.0-GOLDEN. Core feature weights and equations are locked against further hyperparameter adjustments.

Infrastructure Focus: Shift engineering focus to REST API throughput, geospatial query indexing, and deployment reliability.

Empirical Field Validation: Validate the frozen v12.0 architecture against real unseen observational datasets (NASA FIRMS historical active-fire detections, ERA5 reanalysis micro-climates, and Sentinel-2 satellite telemetry) to evaluate whether the frozen v12.0 architecture improves upon the initial ~0.585 empirical paired backtest baseline.
